privacy policy

last updated: august 2026

information we collect

When you place an order through Artasimn, we collect the details you enter at checkout: full name, phone number, e-mail address, and delivery address. We also keep a record of what you ordered, when, and for how much.

how we use it

This information is used only to process and deliver your order, and to contact you about it if something needs your attention. We don't sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal data with third parties, except delivery partners who need your address and phone number to actually get the order to you.

cart & local storage

The items in your cart are stored locally in your own browser (not on our servers) until you complete checkout, so it's remembered between visits on the same device. Clearing your browser's site data will empty it.

your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reach out using the contact details below and we'll take care of it.

contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@artasimn.com.
